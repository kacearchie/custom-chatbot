Pharmacokinetics: ADME Principles

Pharmacokinetics quantifies how the human body processes chemical drugs through four distinct stages: Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion (ADME).

  • Absorption: Movement of drug from route of entry into plasma.
  • Distribution: Dispersion throughout body fluids and tissue spaces.
  • Metabolism: Biotransformation into active or inactive metabolites (primarily via Hepatic CYP450 enzymes).
  • Excretion: Clearance from the body (principally Renal filtration).
